# Adversarial credential publication audit

A context-free opaque-literal inventory found a cookie-shaped regression fixture inherited from the Printing Press JWT-shape template. The generator's older Factor75 retrospective describes that value as a browser visitor/session cookie. It was pre-existing public upstream material, unrelated to the Google Voice account; treating every fixture as invented was inaccurate.

The cookie-shaped fixture is now built from repeated synthetic characters. Both JWT-shaped positive fixtures also use a newly invented header/key identifier plus repeated fake payload and signature bytes. Regression semantics are preserved, with no browser-captured values needed. A local patch record preserves this invariant during regeneration. Historical public commits and other upstream CLIs still retain the prior public fixture; this change does not rewrite history or revoke a credential.

The broader audit covers both Google Voice publication histories, source literals and signed URLs, PR prose, the scope image and metadata, and workflow logs. No Google Voice account credentials, private message samples or private account identifiers have been found. Validation receipts report exercised checks separately from skips.
