### Stubs (explicitly approved, none)
No shipping-scope stub rows. `generate prepare` is not a stub — it fully stages the generation locally; only the final credit-spend click is intentionally left to the user (documented limitation, not a placeholder).

### Known Gaps (documented, not stubs)
- **New-generation submission cannot be fully automated.** `flowCreationAgent:streamChat` requires a fresh reCAPTCHA Enterprise solve on every turn (confirmed by live capture: every single call, including a benign status-check chat message, was followed by a fresh `recaptcha/enterprise/clr` mint). `generate prepare` stages everything and opens the browser at the ready-to-approve step; it does not click Approve for you.
- **Flow cannot ingest or lip-sync to the user's own audio.** No upload accept-list, anywhere in the product, includes an audio MIME type; the "Voices" tab is TTS character-voice casting, not an audio attach point. `mux` exists specifically to close this gap locally, after the fact.
