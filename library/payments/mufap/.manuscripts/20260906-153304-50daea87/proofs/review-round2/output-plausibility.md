## [ERROR]
`freshness` ANCHORS LAG TO A FORWARD-DATED NAV, SO 96% OF THE UNIVERSE IS REPORTED STALE. `mufap-pp-cli freshness --limit 600 --json` (run 2026-09-06) returns newest_validity_date 2026-09-07, modal_validity_date 2026-09-04, lagging_funds 529 of rows_with_validity_date 551. Lag is newest-minus-row (freshness.go:210-226), and the newest date is tomorrow because MUFAP forward-prices 42 funds — a fact the same summary reports as forward_dated_funds. Consequences in the actual row set: the 345 funds carrying the modal, entirely current 2026-09-04 are each emitted with lag_days 3; the 8 funds priced TODAY (validity 2026-09-06) are emitted with lag_days 1. ## [ERROR]
`freshness` SUMMARY IS ARITHMETICALLY IMPOSSIBLE — TWO COUNTS OVERLAP AND OVERFLOW THE UNIVERSE. Repeated stably across runs: rows_with_validity_date 551, forward_dated_funds 42, lagging_funds 529. 42 + 529 = 571 > 551. Cause: the two counts use different anchors in the same JSON object — forward_dated is `o.date > modal` (2026-09-04) at freshness.go:233, lagging is lag >= min-lag-days against `newest` (2026-09-07). The 20 rows at validity 2026-09-05 (12 funds) and 2026-09-06 (8 funds) are counted as forward-dated AND as lagging simultaneously; a fund cannot be both ahead of and behind the market. The dual anchor is intentional (comment at freshness.go:27-29) but nothing in the emitted JSON exposes the overlap, so any consumer that partitions the universe into fresh/stale/forward double-counts 20 funds and overstates the total by 3.6%. Cross-check from the returned rows: 529 enumerated rows span 2026-02-08..2026-09-06, leaving only 22 rows at 2026-09-07 — so 'forward dated' is also not 42-vs-today under any reading a reader would apply.

## [ERROR]
