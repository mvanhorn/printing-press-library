The public package preserves research, source fingerprints, acceptance status, command counts, review findings, security reports and benchmark metrics. Raw historical live response dumps and full live output samples are omitted; the private build archive retains them locally. Host-specific paths are replaced with placeholders. The fresh publish-time raw transcript was kept outside all manuscripts and deleted after the gate.

The full publish-time live gate executed 127 checks successfully, with 97 unsupported rows explicitly skipped/unverified. Source estimates remain separate from confirmed full totals; equipment stock and dated one-way availability remain unknown, with final booking confirmation in Toyota’s human browser flow.

The mechanical sweep found no credential values or customer/workspace PII. Capitalized phrase and email-shaped candidates were reviewed as public API/shop vocabulary, required author attribution, or synthetic unit-test inputs.
