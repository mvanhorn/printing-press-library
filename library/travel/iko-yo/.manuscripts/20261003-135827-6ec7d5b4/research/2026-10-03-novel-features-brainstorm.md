## Customer model

### Mika — infant-and-toddler weekend parent

**Today (without this CLI):** Mika opens a Trip spot page, its basic-information table and the factual paragraphs around it to check whether a child fits a stated age range and whether indoor play, nursing and changing space are explicitly described. The brief's spot 8220 demonstrates that these useful facts can sit outside the table. A family-oriented title cannot answer the missing-facility questions.

**Weekly ritual:** Before choosing the next weekend's outing, Mika checks a few selected places against the child's age and requested amenities, then keeps the source links for the family.

**Frustration:** Repeatedly finding the same scattered facts and distinguishing an unstated amenity from an explicitly supported one.

### Kei — parent planning a regional weekend trip

**Today (without this CLI):** Kei opens Trip's region and prefecture listings, follows pagination and checks event pages against a short trip window. The national event listing contains many archived entries; opening a geographically relevant card does not establish that its published dates overlap the trip.

**Weekly ritual:** Kei searches the next available family weekend in a selected region, checks several events and spots, and revisits the shortlist as plans settle. This follows the official FAQ's weekend and long-weekend parent/child trip scope and the brief's first and third workflows.

**Frustration:** Separating dated candidates from archived or unknown schedules while remembering how much of the listing was actually searched.

### Rina — multigenerational outing organizer

**Today (without this CLI):** Rina keeps several basic-information tables open to compare child/adult charges and reads event paragraphs for application intervals, payment timing and capacity conditions. Spot 8220's adult-price qualifier and event 8412's application interval and lottery demonstrate why a single price or availability label is insufficient.

**Weekly ritual:** Rina checks the family's weekend shortlist for charge differences and application cutoffs before sharing source links or deciding which event to pursue. The brief grounds this in the app's multigenerational planning use case and its third workflow.

**Frustration:** Losing fee qualifiers or confusing a published application window and capacity statement with present seat availability.

1. **Bounded date-window discovery:** Weekly use: Kei runs it when choosing the next family weekend. Wrapper check: it combines pagination, source-window accounting and date classification, which no individual list endpoint supplies. Transcendence proof: Trip's archived-event pattern and a shared spot/event output shape create the value; unknown schedule rows remain visible and unmatched counts refer only to fetched records. Sibling kill: `trip availability` falsely converts published dates into current availability. Buildability: **hand-code**, a Cobra command wired in `root.go` with shared HTML client/normalizers; declare `// pp:data-source live` and reject local mode with a clear no-local-data-source error pointing to `trip cached`. Long validity: `trip compare` and `trip cached` survive under those exact names. Score dimensions: Domain Fit 3, User Pain 2, Build Feasibility 2, Research Backing 2.

2. **Evidence-backed family fact card:** Weekly use: Mika inspects the few shortlisted outings before each weekend; Rina checks one event's conditions before applying. Wrapper check: bounded explicit paragraph extraction, field-level evidence pointers and unknown reasons exceed the absorbed basic-table get commands. Transcendence proof: agent-shaped evidence output preserves the service's scattered factual content without archiving articles. Sibling kill: `trip rank` cannot establish suitability from excluded parent reviews. Buildability: **hand-code**, a Cobra command plus shared exact-pattern extraction with unsupported or ambiguous statements left unknown; declare `// pp:data-source auto` and honor live/local selection and provenance on fallback. Long validity: `trip compare` and `trip discover` survive under those exact names. Score dimensions: 3, 2, 2, 2.

3. **Family-constraint comparison:** Weekly use: Mika checks the next weekend's shortlist against age and facilities, and Kei checks its published schedule against the trip date. Wrapper check: it performs cross-record constraint evaluation rather than renaming a detail route. Transcendence proof: a normalized cross-kind evidence matrix with independent unknown states lets an agent compare facts without interpreting article prose. Sibling kill: `trip rainy-day` needs weather and unsupported suitability inference; its useful indoor predicate survives here. Buildability: **hand-code**, one `trip compare` Cobra file wired in `root.go` using the fact-card normalizer, capped at eight selected detail fetches; declare `// pp:data-source auto` and preserve each row's observation time and data source. Long validity: `trip discover` and `trip inspect` survive under those exact names. Score dimensions: 3, 2, 2, 1.

4. **Qualified charge and application comparison:** Weekly use: Rina checks charges and cutoffs when reviewing the family's weekend options. Wrapper check: it aligns differently structured spot/event charges and compares explicit application boundaries against `--as-of`; it is another behavior of the same `trip compare` command. Transcendence proof: service-specific payment, deadline and lottery facts become agent-shaped comparable evidence, with capacity and seat availability kept distinct. Sibling kill: `trip budget` cannot produce a definitive total from incomplete fee bands and qualifiers. Buildability: **hand-code**, extend the surviving `trip compare` file with shared fee slots and deterministic date-boundary checks; source strategy remains its single `// pp:data-source auto` directive. Do not emit an available-seat field, infer an absent deadline, or interpret an unqualified range as continuous daily operation. Long validity: `trip discover` and `trip inspect` survive under those exact names. Score dimensions: 3, 2, 2, 2.

5. **Offline shortlist with retained coverage:** Weekly use: the named parents revisit their saved shortlist between weekend-planning checks, including without network access. Wrapper check: local fact filtering joined to collection provenance is unavailable from a single remote route and adds domain constraints beyond a generic text search. Transcendence proof: local SQLite and visible observation/coverage evidence support repeatable offline assessment. Sibling kill: `trip changes` adds historical snapshot machinery for a ritual the brief does not establish; current evidence and retained observation timestamps satisfy the stated repeat-check need. Long validity: `trip discover` and `trip compare` survive under those exact names. Score dimensions: 3, 2, 2, 1.
