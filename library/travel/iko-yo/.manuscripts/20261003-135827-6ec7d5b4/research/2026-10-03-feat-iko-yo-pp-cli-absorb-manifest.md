Five hand-code behaviors use four trip intents: discover, inspect, compare and cached. Preserve unknown facts, bounded listing coverage, original observation/publication times and source qualifiers. Reject unsupported data-source modes. No article/review/profile archive, live inventory, inferred seat availability, whole-family price total or extra weather provider. Source date spans indicate published schedule overlap only; uncertain/noncontinuous schedules stay unknown. All geographical examples use observed Kanto region 6 / Saitama prefecture 11.
