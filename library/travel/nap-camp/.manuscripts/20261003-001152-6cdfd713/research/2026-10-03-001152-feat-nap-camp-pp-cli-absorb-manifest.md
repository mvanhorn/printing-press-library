# Nap Camp Absorb Manifest

Public read-only campsite and specific-pitch planning. Bookings and account actions are outside the client scope.

## Absorbed
| # | Feature | Best Source | Our Implementation | Added Value |
|---|---------|-------------|--------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Campervan-entry regional/filter/date discovery | Native public category/list and verified search contract | nap-camp-pp-cli campsite discover | Named filters; bounded results and plan previews; explicit category semantics and date context |
| 2 | Campsite detail, facilities, season and fees | Public campsite object | nap-camp-pp-cli campsite inspect | Canonical URL, Japanese names, labelled unknowns, selected-plan distinction |
| 3 | Specific pitch rules, entry memo, area, capacity, power, pets and starting price | Public plan detail + native modal | nap-camp-pp-cli pitch inspect | Clean text and vehicle/dimension unknowns; source price qualifiers |

| 5 | Region/prefecture and facility filter discovery | Public locations/master hierarchy | nap-camp-pp-cli catalog | Bounded projections, stable IDs, source labels |
