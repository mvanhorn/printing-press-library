## Customer model

### Campervan itinerary planner

**Today (without this CLI):** This traveler opens Nap Camp's campervan category, region lists, campsite details, and individual pitch pages. The category establishes broad discovery eligibility, while the selected pitch can still describe passenger-car entry or other restrictions. The traveler cannot infer their vehicle's permitted dimensions from category membership.

**Weekly ritual:** During trip planning, refresh a bounded regional shortlist, inspect selected pitches, and compare services and unresolved requirements before handing off to the booking page.

**Frustration:** Campsite-level amenities and vehicle categories can hide a selected pitch's restrictions, leaving the same evidence scattered across tabs.

### Traveler coordinating dated campsite stays

**Today (without this CLI):** This traveler opens each shortlisted pitch's calendar and checks overnight dates against the source legend. Preparation, accepting, limited, ended, and cancellation-notification states require different interpretations. A price-from label cannot establish the party's complete booked total.

**Weekly ritual:** Recheck prospective dates and consecutive nights while rearranging the campsite portion of an itinerary.

**Frustration:** Repeated calendar inspection makes it easy to confuse arrival-date acceptance with vacancy, to include a checkout date as another night, or to treat a zero sentinel as a free stay.

### Itinerary evidence keeper

**Today (without this CLI):** This traveler copies facility and pitch details into a small local shortlist and revisits the source when dates, rules, or starting prices change. They need stable IDs, source links, timestamps, and explicit coverage to understand what an observation proves.

**Weekly ritual:** Save a fresh bounded observation of the chosen shortlist, compare it with the previous observation, and update unanswered checks before booking directly.

**Frustration:** Manually comparing revisited pages loses the distinction between source changes, changed requested coverage, and previously unknown information.

**Selected-pitch requirement check:** Weekly use: the itinerary planner rechecks the selected pitch as vehicle/service requirements change during active planning. Wrapper vs leverage: combines facility and plan evidence with user requirements rather than returning one endpoint. Transcendence proof: cross-entity join plus an agent-shaped tri-state checklist. Sibling kill: `planner vehicle-match` promises dimensions the source cannot prove. Buildability: `hand-code`; a Cobra command and shared typed evidence normalizer must implement requirement checks. Long-description validity: `planner compare` survives under that exact path. No global “fits” verdict may override unknown vehicle dimensions, unverified restrictions, or a missing full quote.

**Evidence shortlist comparison:** Weekly use: the itinerary planner refreshes the chosen shortlist during the repeated comparison ritual. Wrapper vs leverage: synthesizes multiple selected campsite/pitch pairs, not one search endpoint. Transcendence proof: bounded cross-entity fan-out and consistent agent-shaped evidence columns. Sibling kill: `planner facilities` duplicates filters and collapses facility versus pitch evidence. Buildability: `hand-code`; reuse the checklist normalizer and bound the number of pairs and public requests. Long-description validity: `planner windows` survives under that exact path. Preserve each pair's canonical links and rejected/unknown requirements; do not invent a subjective ranking.

**Consecutive acceptance windows:** Weekly use: the dated-stay traveler rechecks consecutive nights when rearranging the itinerary. Wrapper vs leverage: groups multiple source dates into a requested-night candidate result; `pitch calendar` remains the raw dated evidence workflow. Transcendence proof: the service-specific status legend plus deterministic overnight-window calculation. Sibling kill: `planner calendar` is only a renamed endpoint. Buildability: `hand-code`; calendar normalization and bounded consecutive-night computation need custom Go. Long-description validity: `planner fit` survives under that exact path. For check-in D and checkout D+N, evaluate N nights, not the checkout date as an additional arrival. Exclude status 0/3/4 from acceptance candidates, retain missing dates as unknown, and preserve starting-price qualifiers.

**Dated evidence snapshot:** Weekly use: the evidence keeper saves the latest chosen scope before updating the local shortlist. Wrapper vs leverage: produces an observation spanning related entities with local provenance and coverage, not one API response. Transcendence proof: cross-entity evidence persistence designed for subsequent local comparison. Sibling kill: `planner total` fabricates a complete dated quote from starting-price evidence. Buildability: `hand-code`; save a small versioned JSON schema with bounded collection lengths and atomic local output. Long-description validity: `planner changes` survives under that exact path. Reuse normalized source evidence, document freshness, and never claim saved observations are current availability.

**Observation drift comparison:** Weekly use: the evidence keeper compares the latest weekly source recheck to the previous observation. Wrapper vs leverage: no remote endpoint computes these local observation differences. Transcendence proof: cross-observation local query, ignoring metadata-only timestamp changes and preserving different coverage as a separate condition. Sibling kill: `planner recommend` substitutes opaque preference judgment for auditable facts. Buildability: `hand-code`; compare bounded typed observations and emit initialized empty change arrays on no change. Long-description validity: `planner snapshot` survives under that exact path. Validate schema and entity IDs; report comparable domain-field changes and explicitly disclose dates covered by only one input.
