## Customer model

**Itinerary agent assembling Japan trip candidates**

**Today (without this CLI):** Opens Walkerplus prefecture, city, category and month listings, then event pages to reconcile a travel interval with each current edition. The native date controls do not express an arbitrary ISO date range or year.

**Weekly ritual:** Builds and revises bounded city-by-city event shortlists for travelers, preserving Japanese names and enough source facts for a later choice.

**Frustration:** Repeatedly checking dates and duplicate cards without knowing whether a broad event range actually covers the requested days.

**Traveler checking a shortlist before departure**

**Today (without this CLI):** Reopens the selected event's overview, data and price pages to check the edition, closures, hours, admission and reservation wording. Overall dates and JSON-LD offers do not answer all these questions.

**Weekly ritual:** During active trip preparation, revisits shortlisted events and checks the schedule and practical constraints before committing a day to them.

**Frustration:** Details are scattered, and an event's date envelope can conceal a closure or an unresolved schedule.

**Traveler choosing a free or indoor alternative**

**Today (without this CLI):** Browses events for a planned day and inspects Japanese source descriptions and displayed admission facts. A missing or misleading structured price cannot establish that admission is free, and a category cannot establish that the venue is indoors.

**Weekly ritual:** During trip planning and travel, finds alternatives for a city and date under an admission or indoor constraint.

**Frustration:** Rechecking every candidate's evidence while resisting unsupported assumptions about missing attributes.

## Candidates (pre-cut)

All candidates are confined to Walkerplus public HTML and its event JSON-LD. Derived fields remain separate from source facts. Discovery and detail requests must obey explicit page, request and concurrency bounds; bounded HTTP caching is already absorbed. Listing JSON-LD can supply full dates and location without detail fetches; its URL is an organizer URL, so stable identity comes from Walkerplus cards. Approximate displayed seasonal wording overrides exact-looking schema dates for certainty. No SQLite, background sync, booking, provider expansion or LLM dependency is proposed. There is no prior research manifest, so reprint reconciliation does not apply. The brief contains User Vision but no Codebase Intelligence section.
