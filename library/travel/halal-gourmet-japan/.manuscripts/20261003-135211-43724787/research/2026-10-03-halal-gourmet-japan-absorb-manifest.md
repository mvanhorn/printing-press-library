## Scope contract
- Full-detail snapshots only for local planning commands; at most 20 IDs per kind and 400 possible food/prayer coordinate pairs.
- Certification label, certifier/validity evidence, HGJ verification month and each food/facility condition are independent fields. Missing detail evidence is not-reported; hidden card evidence is not-visible-on-card; explicit entity mismatch is inapplicable.
- Search filters are discovery semantics, not a proof that all requested conditions match. Strict matching evaluates each full-detail label locally.
- Every novel command rejects --data-source live with no live equivalent. Empty or uninspected local selections return explicit gaps instead of fabricated facts.
- Two latest successful factual detail versions per entity; only bounded scoped reads. No reviews/profiles/session state/signed media, religious certainty, bookings, ingredient scanning, current-opening promise, routes or complete inventory claim.
