## Scope limitations
- Historical dates are recorded first/last endpoints; elapsed spans do not prove uninterrupted operating days or future openings.
- No current lift-status product; the daily snow-depth chart is not yet released in October 2026.
- Reports cover named regions and use reporter base/town measurements; new snowfall is since the previous report, not necessarily 24 hours.
- Source narratives, reviews and private/session data are excluded.
- Five hand-written local computations, zero spec-emitted novel computations, zero stubs.

## Discovery scope revision
Popular-region membership is client rendered and absent from the replayable national chart/region SSR. It is excluded from the shipping command surface. Individual resort pages retain published popular_region labels; they are not claimed as a complete regional membership list. This routine evidence-based scope choice is preauthorized. All nine absorbed command paths and five independently brainstormed local computations remain useful and unchanged; the novelty input data and personas did not change.
