## Domain guarantees and limits
Date search is provider-filtered candidacy, availability remains unknown. Prices are JPY starting references/night, not complete quotes. Parking dimensions are meters; dimensional fit is not vehicle acceptance. Facilities absent or lacking exist are unknown; false plus notification needs confirmation. Source on-site restroom and nearby toilet remain separate. Original Japanese names, stable IDs, canonical URLs, source-language coverage, JST dates and observation time survive normalization. Bound lists, scans and shortlist detail calls separately; all empty lists are [].
