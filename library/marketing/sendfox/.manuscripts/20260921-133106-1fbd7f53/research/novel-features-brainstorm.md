## Customer model

**Weekly newsletter creator.**

**Today (without this CLI):** The creator moves between a local newsletter draft, SendFox campaign configuration, audience lists, sender domains, and performance pages. The brief identifies separate stats, link, cohort, and suppression reads as the work needed to decide whether another message is justified. A campaign's headline open rate alone does not answer which eligible people a resend would reach.

**Weekly ritual:** Prepare one newsletter, check its audience and sender, leave a reviewable draft, then inspect the previous issue's engagement and consider a resend.

**Frustration:** There is no single evidence packet connecting draft configuration, audience exclusions, sender readiness, and the later engagement decision.

**Audience operations assistant.**

**Today (without this CLI):** The assistant compares subscriber CSVs, contact records, list membership, tags, custom fields, and unsubscribe records. The prior CLI offered CSV auditing and a live hygiene report, but the new contract exposes more relationships and scoped contact activity. The brief warns that a contact update can replace all list memberships and that a root-resource export does not prove child data is complete.

**Weekly ritual:** Audit new/imported subscribers and suppression differences, investigate individual subscriber questions, and export evidence for other work.

**Frustration:** A clean-looking count can hide missing memberships, stale child data, duplicate identities, or a subscriber who should remain suppressed.

**Creator-business automation maintainer.**

**Today (without this CLI):** The maintainer compares a versioned welcome-sequence file with an automation, its trigger, and individual email items. SendFox documents list/open/click triggers and integer delays, but the public API does not expose complete enrollment/progress inspection. Activation also reschedules stale deliverables, so an apparently ordinary update can affect delivery.

**Weekly ritual:** Review the welcome sequence alongside the weekly content cycle, revise content or delays when needed, and verify that intended draft/inactive state is preserved.

**Frustration:** Translating a sequence into several dependent API operations obscures ordering, defaults, and the point at which real sending can begin.

**Cathryn as platform evaluator.**

**Today (without this CLI):** Cathryn compares Kit export files and SendFox's documented capabilities while deciding whether to change platforms. She needs normal SendFox operations to be excellent first. A field/tag/list mapping is insufficient when suppression, sequence progress, public content, or events have no equivalent public API.

**Weekly ritual:** During evaluation, repeat snapshot comparisons and late-change audits; review explicit parity blockers before considering a pilot or cutover.

**Frustration:** A migration-shaped checklist can look complete while silently omitting unsubscribes, missing source fields, or an API gap that prevents parity.

Parse, output, close, and write errors are fatal rather than silently producing incomplete evidence.
