# Traffic Intel research

Private composite CLI designed as an operator layer above existing Printing Press child CLIs:

- Google Search Console: page/query search analytics
- Google Analytics 4: page sessions, conversions, and revenue
- Ahrefs: top pages, traffic estimates, keywords, and referring domains

The source CLIs remain the API-specific adapters. `traffic-intel-pp-cli` owns the normalized local store and cross-source commands for money pages, query revenue, drop explanation, refresh queues, and weekly digests.
